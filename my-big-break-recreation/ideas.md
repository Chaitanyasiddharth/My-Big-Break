# My Big Break Recreation — Design Ground Truth

## Reference
The supplied reference is https://mybigbreak.in/. This project should reproduce the public homepage content, information hierarchy, service offerings, navigation labels, FAQ copy, contact form fields, footer links, and overall blue/white corporate visual language. Fidelity to the reference overrides exploratory redesign. The recreation may improve spacing, responsive behavior, accessibility, and interaction polish without changing the public claims or service information.

## Chosen Direction: Reference-Matched Career Advancement Editorial

### Design Movement
Early-2020s Indian professional-services web design: bright white canvas, deep corporate blue, sharp typography, oversized service panels, and restrained lime-green accent details.

### Core Principles
1. Preserve the source site's plain-spoken, career-focused information architecture and service language.
2. Use a bright, high-contrast, trustworthy visual system with blue as the authority color and green as a small progress/action accent.
3. Translate the source's long vertical scroll into clearly separated, editorial sections with generous whitespace and strong alignment.
4. Keep every public-facing label and service detail from the reference visible, while making the page responsive and keyboard-friendly.

### Color Philosophy
Deep navy-blue communicates credibility, expertise, and recruitment confidence. White keeps the service content open and approachable. A fresh lime green echoes the source site's tiny highlight details and represents momentum, action, and a "big break" moment. Warm off-white surfaces can distinguish sections without diluting the bright source aesthetic.

### Layout Paradigm
A long-form editorial landing page with alternating image/text bands, an offset hero composition, stacked service blocks, and a high-visibility contact conversion panel. Avoid a generic centered marketing template: use split compositions and asymmetry while retaining the source's sequence of Resume Writing → Interview Coaching → Assessments → FAQ → Contact.

### Signature Elements
- Blue section labels with lime-green marker ticks.
- Large uppercase headings paired with compact explanatory copy.
- Resume-paper, profile-card, and assessment motifs used as lightweight visual cues.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel helpful and direct: navigation anchors scroll to the exact service area, CTAs focus the contact form, FAQ rows expand in place, and form submission gives an honest inline success state without pretending to send real data.

### Animation
Use short, subtle reveal motion for section content and a restrained lift on cards and buttons. Keep animations under 300ms, use transform/opacity only, and respect prefers-reduced-motion. No decorative motion should compete with the service copy.

### Typography System
Use a distinctive display face for headings and a highly legible sans-serif for body copy. Headings are compact, uppercase or title case, with tight tracking and strong weight. Body copy is 16–18px on desktop with relaxed line-height. Avoid Inter and generic all-purpose typography.

### Brand Essence
Practical career storytelling for professionals who want their next opportunity to recognize their real potential. Personality: **credible, encouraging, precise**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are clear, confident, and outcome-led. CTAs are action-oriented but not pushy. Microcopy is reassuring and specific.

Example lines:
- "Professionally written, by experts who know your industry."
- "Sharpen your interview skills with our experienced & finest coaches."

### Wordmark & Logo
Use the provided source logo as a visual reference for a compact blue-and-white badge with a lime accent and an italic handwritten-style tagline. In the recreated interface, use a simple symbol mark and text lockup rather than attempting to redraw tiny source lettering inaccurately.

### Signature Brand Color
My Big Break Blue — #123D8D — used for primary headings, navigation emphasis, buttons, and large section fields.

## Style Decisions
- Ground-truth replication is prioritized over adding unrelated sections or invented proof points.
- Use generated visuals only as supportive decorative assets; keep the content and hierarchy faithful to the reference site.
